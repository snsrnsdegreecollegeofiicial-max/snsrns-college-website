About the College

Rooted in Values,
Rising with Excellence

SNSRNS Degree College was established in 2022 with a mission to bring quality higher education to the heart of rural Bihar. Situated at Village Dermahan (Darmaha), Post Kesariya, Block Kalyanpur, District East Champaran, the college is dedicated to the academic, social, and personal development of every student.

We are permanently affiliated to B.R. Ambedkar Bihar University (BRABU), Muzaffarpur, vide affiliation letter No. 14/ए0एफ0-21/2023-1054 dated 28 February 2025, ensuring that all degrees and certifications are fully recognised under the Bihar Government's academic framework.
